# Note lengths in real flue music

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** a quick web search, not a survey. Most tempo figures come from forums, tune sites and community transcriptions; treat them as typical ranges, not standards.

## Question

How short are the notes in music people actually play on ocarina, tin whistle, flute and recorder? This sets the targets for grading short notes in timed songs ([F09](../features/09-timed-scoring/design.md)).

## Findings

Tempo figures for jigs and reels count two beats a bar: the dotted quarter in a jig, the half note in a reel. The note length is the shortest common melody note, before ornaments.

| Music | Tempo (as counted) | Shortest common note | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner songs, folk tunes, ocarina song books | ♩ = 80–120 (assumed; the books found give no tempos) | eighth | 375–250 ms |
| Irish jig, learning tempo | dotted ♩ = 80–100 | eighth | 250–200 ms |
| Irish reel, slow session | half = 70–80 | eighth | 214–188 ms |
| Song of Storms (a game tune popular on ocarina) | ♩ = 128–160 (transcriptions disagree) | eighth | 234–188 ms |
| Irish jig, session | dotted ♩ = 100–130 | eighth | 200–154 ms |
| Handel recorder sonata HWV 369, Allegro | ♩ = 100 (one tune site) | sixteenth | 150 ms |
| Irish reel, session | half = 90–120 | eighth | 167–125 ms |
| Irish reel, dance tempo | half = 120–140 | eighth | 125–107 ms |

- Hornpipes were left out: the figures found disagree, and their dotted rhythm makes the short note of each pair shorter than the table's even eighths would suggest.
- Ornaments (cuts, taps, rolls, grace notes) are much shorter than the melody notes. No figure was found.
- Sessions vary widely; whoever starts the tune sets the tempo. Learners are advised to practise slowly with a metronome, often around 70–80.

## What it means

Three groups emerge:

- **Songs and beginner material:** notes of 250 ms and longer.
- **Slow-session dance tunes, game tunes, baroque at a moderate tempo:** notes down to about 190 ms.
- **Session-speed reels and jigs, fast baroque passages:** notes of 125–150 ms, and shorter at dance tempo.
