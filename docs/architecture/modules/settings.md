# settings

`src/core/settings/index.ts`

## Purpose

The player's settings (range, sharps flag, reveal delay, auto-advance and its delay, play note with its delay and duration, judge and its tolerance, smart sampling, the mode and practice with Single note's two delays, and the song's ABC text) as validated data, plus the sharps snapping rule and the sound sum rule. It knows nothing of the defaults file or of storage; the app reads those and passes the values in.

## Public interface

- `interface Settings { lowest: Pitch; highest: Pitch; includeSharps: boolean; delaySeconds: number; autoAdvance: boolean; advanceDelaySeconds: number; soundEnabled: boolean; soundDelaySeconds: number; soundDurationSeconds: number; judgeEnabled: boolean; smartSampling: boolean; toleranceCents: number; mode: Mode; practice: Practice; fingeringDelaySeconds: number; singleAdvanceDelaySeconds: number; metronome: boolean; scoring: Scoring; barsPerLine: number; songAbc: string; songTune: number }`. `songTune` (F14-S01) is the chosen tune's 1-based position in the song text, checked by `isValidSongTune` (a whole number of at least 1); anything else resets alone, and a missing one takes the default. Whether the text has that many tunes is the app's job: this module never parses ABC. `barsPerLine` is how many bars each line of a timed song's staff holds, a whole number from `MIN_BARS_PER_LINE` (1) to `MAX_BARS_PER_LINE` (4), checked by `isValidBarsPerLine`; anything else resets alone, and a missing one takes the default. `soundEnabled` is play note; `soundDelaySeconds` is how long after the reveal the note starts and `soundDurationSeconds` how long it sounds. `delaySeconds` is the reveal delay; `advanceDelaySeconds` is how long a revealed card waits before the next one appears when `autoAdvance` is on. `mode` (F16-S01) is the Mode list: `Mode` is `"flashcards" | "song"`, checked by `isMode`; it replaced the use song toggle. `practice` is the Practice list: `Practice` is `"single" | "tempo"`, checked by `isPractice`; it replaced the keep tempo toggle. Both are kept as set whether or not they apply (a hidden Tempo practice stays while the mode is Flashcards). `fingeringDelaySeconds` is how long Single note waits before showing the fingering and `singleAdvanceDelaySeconds` how long after that before the next note with the microphone off; both are finite numbers in [0.5, 30], the same bounds as the reveal delay, and nothing uses them until F16-S02. `metronome` the metronome toggle (F08-S04: a click on every beat of a timed song, kept as set whether or not it applies) `scoring` the Scoring list (F12-S01: `Scoring` is `"live" | "after"`, checked by `isScoring`; whether a timed song's judging shows as it plays or after the song, kept as set whether or not it applies) and `songAbc` the player's tune as typed; this module checks the type and length of both and never parses ABC (the [abc](abc.md) adapter does, in the app). `smartSampling` is the "Use smart sampling" checkbox (F06-S02), kept as set whether or not it applies; `judgeEnabled` turns microphone judging on; `toleranceCents` is how far from the expected pitch a played tone may be and still be correct.
- `interface SettingsJson`: the serialized form shared by the defaults file and storage. Pitches are note names (`A3`, `C♯5`), like the instrument JSON.
- `toSettingsJson(settings): SettingsJson`.
- `MIN_DELAY_SECONDS` (0.5), `MAX_DELAY_SECONDS` (30); the reveal and advance delays use these bounds. `MIN_SOUND_DELAY_SECONDS` (0) and `MIN_SOUND_DURATION_SECONDS` (0.5) are the sound minimums; both sound values share the maximum of 30. `MIN_TOLERANCE_CENTS` (10) and `MAX_TOLERANCE_CENTS` (45) bound the tolerance, below 50 so a tone nearer the neighbouring semitone is never correct.
- `MAX_SONG_CHARS` (10000): the longest song text kept; the abc adapter's `MAX_ABC_CHARS` is this constant.
- `isValidTolerance(value)`: a finite number from 10 to 45 inclusive.
- `type SettingsResult`: `{ ok: true, settings } | { ok: false, reason }`. `SharpsResult` is an alias of it.
- `class SettingsError`.
- `snapToNaturals(lowest, highest)`: a sharp low bound moves up a semitone, a sharp high bound moves down; `null` when `lowest >= highest` results.
- `setIncludeSharps(settings, on)`: `{ ok: true, settings }` or `{ ok: false, reason }`. On keeps the bounds; off snaps, and is blocked when snapping leaves no range.
- `canTurnSharpsOff(settings): boolean`.
- **Sound sum rule:** with play note and auto-advance both on, the sound delay plus the sound duration must not exceed the advance delay (the tone ends before the next card). Helpers: `soundRuleApplies(s)`, `soundFits(s)` (the sum compared with a 1e-9 tolerance, so 0.5 + 1.5 against 2 passes), `breaksSoundRule(s)` and `soundTooLongReason(s)` (`"Sound delay plus duration (2 s) is longer than the advance delay (1 s)"`).
- `canTurnSoundOn(s)`, `canTurnAutoAdvanceOn(s)`, `setSoundEnabled(s, on)` and `setAutoAdvance(s, on)`: turning on is `{ ok: false, reason: soundTooLongReason }` when the result would break the rule; turning off always succeeds.
- `setTimings(s, patch)`: `patch` is a partial of `soundDelaySeconds`, `soundDurationSeconds` and `advanceDelaySeconds`. Returns `{ ok: false, reason: "The sound delay plus the sound duration must not exceed the advance delay." }` when the result breaks the rule, otherwise the new settings. No rule applies while play note or auto-advance is off.
- `validateSettings(raw, instrument, defaults): Settings`: never throws. Order:
  1. Each field alone: a non-object, a missing or wrongly typed field, a pitch name `parsePitch` rejects, a non-boolean `autoAdvance` or `soundEnabled`, a delay (reveal or advance) not finite in [0.5, 30], a sound delay not finite in [0, 30] a sound duration not finite in [0.5, 30], a non-boolean `judgeEnabled` or `smartSampling` or a tolerance failing `isValidTolerance` resets to its default. Data saved before judging gets judge off and 40 cents; data saved before smart sampling gets it off with every other field kept.
  Likewise a `mode`, `practice`, `fingeringDelaySeconds` or `singleAdvanceDelaySeconds` that is invalid resets alone to its default, and a `metronome` that is not a boolean does too, and `songAbc` that is not a string or is over `MAX_SONG_CHARS`, reset alone; a missing field (data saved before songs) takes the default, so the metronome is off. **Old data (F16, AC13):** with no valid `mode`, a boolean `useSong` gives it (`true` is song, `false` flashcards); with no valid `practice`, a boolean `keepTempo` gives it (`true` is tempo, `false` single). With neither field the defaults apply (flashcards, single). `keepTempo` sets the practice whenever it is a boolean, even with use song off, so a hidden keep tempo carries over as a hidden Tempo practice. `useSong` and `keepTempo` are read only here and never written back. A `scoring` that is not `"live"` or `"after"` resets alone to the default, and a missing one (data saved before F12) takes it, so scoring is live. A `songAbc` of `""` also takes `defaults.songAbc`, as does a missing one, so data saved before F05 and a cleared box both load as the example song; a non-empty stored text is kept as is. Song mode with no valid song is the app's job.
  2. Each bound is clamped into the instrument's written range.
  3. `lowest >= highest` resets both bounds to the defaults.
  4. With sharps off, the bounds are snapped; if that is blocked, the bounds and the flag reset to the defaults.
  5. If the result breaks the sound sum rule, the sound delay, sound duration and advance delay all reset to the defaults; every other field is kept.
- `smartSamplingApplies(settings): boolean`: `smartSampling && judgeEnabled && mode === "flashcards"`, the one test for whether cards are drawn by need (used from F06-S04). A song never turns it off, it simply is not drawn from the pool.
- `tempoApplies(settings): boolean`: `mode === "song" && practice === "tempo"`, the one test for whether the song is played in time (F08-S02, renamed from `keepTempoApplies` in F16).
- `singleNoteApplies(settings): boolean`: `mode === "song" && practice === "single"` (F16).
- `metronomeApplies(settings): boolean`: `tempoApplies(settings) && metronome` (F08-S04).
- `scoringAfterApplies(settings): boolean`: `tempoApplies(settings) && judgeEnabled && scoring === "after"` (F12-S01).
- `parseDefaults(raw, instrument): Settings`: strict. Throws `SettingsError` naming the field when anything is missing, wrong, out of range, or would be changed by validation. That includes a missing or invalid `mode`, `practice`, `fingeringDelaySeconds` or `singleAdvanceDelaySeconds` (it does not read `useSong` or `keepTempo`), a non-boolean `metronome`, a `scoring` that is not `"live"` or `"after"`, a non-string `songAbc`, a `songTune` that is missing or not a whole number of at least 1 ("songTune must be a whole number of at least 1"), and a `songAbc` over `MAX_SONG_CHARS` (the text may be empty or a song; the shipped file holds the example song, with mode flashcards), and `autoAdvance`, `advanceDelaySeconds`, `soundEnabled`, `soundDelaySeconds`, `soundDurationSeconds`, `judgeEnabled`, `smartSampling` and `toleranceCents`, a `barsPerLine` that is not a whole number from 1 to 4, and a file that breaks the sound sum rule.

## Dependencies

`../pitch` and `../instrument` only. No JSON import, no storage, no framework or browser API.

## Tests

- `tests/core/settings.test.ts`: every rule and edge case above against the alto C instrument (the sound fields' bounds and wrong types, the sum rule at equal and one step over, the toggles, `setTimings`, strict parse; the judge fields' bounds, wrong types, NaN and infinities, each resetting alone, old data without them; the smart sampling default, strict parse, old data, wrong type and the `smartSamplingApplies` truth table; mode and practice (F16-S01): the defaults, the strict parse of each new field (missing and invalid), each resetting alone, the three old-data cases plus neither field, the round trip and the `tempoApplies` and `singleNoteApplies` truth tables; metronome the same, with the `metronomeApplies` truth table; scoring: the default, strict parse, old data, a bad value resetting alone, the round trip and the `scoringAfterApplies` truth table), and that the source has no `.json` import.
- `tests/core/core-imports.test.ts` covers this module's imports and globals.
