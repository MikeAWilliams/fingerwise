# F19: Tuner mode

Feature: [`19-tuner-mode`](../docs/features/19-tuner-mode/design.md)

One section per story, in dependency order. Each story is one commit that leaves the repo building, linted and passing tests.

`fw-execute` runs straight through both stories without stopping for hand checks. Every check by hand is in [`F19-checks.md`](F19-checks.md) and is done once, at Closing.

**Shared shapes.** S01 builds them and S02 fills them in:
- `src/app/tuner-flow.ts`: `createTunerFlow({ listen, onChange })` with `start()`, `step()` and `stop()`, as the card and Single note flows have. `TunerState` is `{ listening: Listening; note: TunerNote | null; held: boolean }`. `TunerNote` is `{ pitch: Pitch; cents: number }` in **written** pitch. S01 always gives `note: null, held: false`.
- `TunerListen` is `{ listener, now }` in S01. S02 adds `watch` (a `ToneWatch`), `follow(readings)` and `hold(readings)`. The last two return `TunerNote | null` and are built in `App` from the core helpers plus the transposition, as `singleListenFor` builds `grade`. The flow never sees sounding pitch or the instrument.
- `Card` gets a `tuner?: TunerState` prop. When it is set, Card draws the tuner's reveal area instead of a flashcard's.

## F19-S02: Show the note played

Story: [`F19-S02-show-the-note`](../docs/features/19-tuner-mode/stories/F19-S02-show-the-note.md)

Covers: AC1, AC2, AC3, AC4, AC5, AC6, AC7, AC8

- [ ] **Core helpers** (`src/core/judge/index.ts`), pure, in sounding pitch, over the readings of one tone. They skip readings that fail `passesChecks(reading, checks)`.
  - `followNote(readings, checks, settleMs): { pitch: Pitch; cents: number; since: number } | null`. The first shown note is the nearest pitch to the median frequency of the passing readings in the first `settleMs`. A candidate run starts at a passing reading whose nearest pitch differs from the shown note. It is reset by a passing reading back on the shown note, or on a third note, which starts its own run. When a run spans at least `settleMs` (newest time minus first), its pitch becomes the shown note, and `since` is the run's first index (AC4). `cents` is `centsFrom` the latest passing reading to the shown note, so it can go beyond ±50 (AC3, AC4). The result is null when no reading passes.
  - `heldNote(readings, checks, settleMs): { pitch; cents } | null`. This is `followNote`'s final note, and the median of `centsFrom` over the passing readings from `since` on (AC5).
  - `withinTolerance(cents, toleranceCents): boolean`: `|cents| <= tolerance + 1e-6`. `judge` is refactored to use it, so the two agree.
- [ ] Tests in `tests/core/judge.test.ts`, with reading sequences at 16.7 ms steps as the story's Test approach lists: a steady tone; a tone at +45¢; a bend across +50¢ held for less than 200 ms (the note is kept and the cents go beyond 50) and for longer (the note changes, and `since` points at the bend's start); a scoop at the attack; a release sag shorter than the held part (the median ignores it); trailing silent readings (skipped); and no passing reading (null). Also `withinTolerance` at the boundary (40 within, 40.01 not), and that `judge`'s existing tests still pass.
- [ ] **Watch wiring in App** (`App.tsx`): a `tunerListen()` gives `{ listener, watch: createToneWatch(TONE_WATCH_CHECKS), now, follow, hold }`. `follow` and `hold` call `followNote` and `heldNote` with `CHECKS` and `DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS.settleMs`, then convert the pitch to written (`pitch - instrument.transposition`, as `judgedNote` does) (AC2). The band in `CHECKS` gives AC1's "outside the band shows nothing". The watch's settle time gives "shorter sounds show nothing".
- [ ] **Tuner flow** (`tuner-flow.ts`): on `"running"`, `watch.reset(now())`. `onReading` pushes each reading to the watch. On `start`, it clears the hold and shows `follow(tone.readings)` with `held: false` (AC7). While a tone is active, it shows `follow(watch.tone().readings)` only when `createReadoutThrottle()` allows it for `reading.time`, so at most every `READOUT_INTERVAL_MS` (AC3). On `end`, it shows `hold(event.readings)` with `held: true` at once (AC5). Silence after the end changes nothing until the next start. It catches errors from the watch and the helpers, as the other flows do. A reset after `stop` is not needed.
- [ ] Tests in `tests/app/tuner-flow.test.ts` with a fake listener and fake `follow` and `hold`, or with the real watch and fake readings at 16.7 ms steps: nothing before a start; a start shows the note; live updates no more often than every 100 ms (count the `onChange` calls over 1 s of readings); an end shows the hold and keeps it through silence; a new tone after a hold follows again (AC3, AC5, AC7).

Otherwise it reads "N cents flat" or "N cents sharp", with N = `Math.round(|cents|)` and "1 cent" singular (AC6). ## Notes

Assumptions (minor, chosen here, open to change at acceptance):
- **The first shown note** of a tone is the nearest note to the median of its first 200 ms, not its first reading, so a scoop at the attack does not show the wrong note.
- **"Readings on the shown note"** (S02 AC5) means the passing readings from the moment the final shown note's run began, including any bend beyond ±50¢ that never settled. It does not mean only the readings whose nearest pitch is that note.
- **Microphone help in Tuner** is a new `TUNER_HELP` line in place of `JUDGE_HELP`, which describes the hidden checkbox. `micSummary` reads `within 40¢` in Tuner.
- **Off-tune row** has no icon: the text says flat or sharp, so colour is not the only signal. Its amber is darker than the lane's, to meet 4.5:1 for text.
- **The empty staff** before the first tone needs `drawStaff` to accept a null pitch. That is a small change to `render/staff`, which the design's Modules touched list leaves out.
- **Out-of-range notes** use staff options widened to the band. Whether VexFlow's 220x150 box shows an octave beyond the range (A2 to F6 written) without clipping is a hand check. If it clips, report it at acceptance rather than resizing the staff here.
- The tone watch has no hold-off in Tuner (`TONE_WATCH_CHECKS`), as in Single note: no reference tone plays. A tone held while Tuner opens only counts after a silence.
