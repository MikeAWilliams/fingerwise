## F12-S03: Listening past the end

Story: [`F12-S03-listening-past-the-end`](../docs/features/12-score-after-the-song/stories/F12-S03-listening-past-the-end.md)

Covers: AC1, AC2, AC3, AC4, AC5, AC6, AC7, AC8, AC9
- [x] New `src/app/overrun.ts` (plain TypeScript): `createSilenceWatch()` with `push(timeMs, sounding)` and `silentMs()`, the song time from the first non-sounding reading after the last sounding one to the newest reading. With no sound at all, it counts from the first reading; with no readings, it is 0. A reading covers the time until the next one, as in coverage. Tests in `tests/app/overrun.test.ts`: always silent, sound then silence, a gap under 1 s then sound resetting it, no readings.
- [x] `src/app/timed-flow.ts`: one constants block, `OVERRUN_SILENCE_MS = 1000` and `OVERRUN_CAP_MS = 10000` (AC9). With `after` and a running scoring:
  - Every reading in song time goes to a `createSilenceWatch` on the scoring, sounding when `passesChecks(reading, listen.coverage.checks)` ([judge](../docs/architecture/modules/judge.md)), the core's silence test (AC1).
  - Readings and envelope blocks whose song time is after the last slot's end (`lastEndMs`, which is the last note's end) are not pushed to the coverage run. Readings are kept in a list on the scoring instead, so the result is what it would be without them (AC6).
  - `TimedState` gains `overrun: boolean`, true from the last slot's end until the summary (phase stays `playing`, so the scroll lock and the following are unchanged), and `overrunReadings: readonly CoverageReading[]`, the kept readings, set only with the summary in `after` and empty otherwise (AC5, AC6).
- [x] The stop rule, in `show()` for the last note when `after && scoring`:
  - At `songEnd`, set `overrun` and emit, then `at(songEnd + lag)`.
  - When that fires, summarise at once if `silentMs() >= OVERRUN_SILENCE_MS` (AC3). Otherwise set `lagPassed`, re-arm the one timer slot with `at(songEnd + (lag + OVERRUN_CAP_MS) / 1000, summarise)` (AC2), and let `onReading` check the watch after each push, calling `cancel()` and `summarise()` once the silence is long enough (AC1).
  - `summarise()` guards against a second call, and time comes only from readings (story notes).
  - Without `after`, or with no scoring, the end is as now (AC8).
  - The metronome is already scheduled only before `songEnd`; nothing is added (AC4).
- [x] `stop()` and `run()` clear the kept readings and the watch with the scoring; `stop()` already cancels the timer, so a stop during the overrun judges nothing (AC7).
- [x] `src/app/judge-text.ts`: `LISTENING_PAST_END` ("Listening… stop playing to see your score"). `App` passes `timedNote` as `timed.overrun ? LISTENING_PAST_END : SCORING_AFTER` in `after` (AC5).
- [x] `tests/app/timed-flow.test.ts`, on the fake audio clock with readings every 16.7 ms:
  - silent at the end ends at `songEnd + lag` (AC3);
  - playing 2 s past the end then silence ends 1 s after the sound stops, in song time (AC1);
  - silence under 1 s then more sound does not end (AC1);

  - no clicks after the last beat with the metronome on, across the overrun (AC4);
  - `overrun` true from `songEnd` until the summary (AC5);
  - `overrunReadings` holds exactly the readings after `lastEndMs`, and `summary` deep-equals a run fed the same readings with those removed (AC6);
  - a `stop()` during the overrun emits nothing and judges nothing (AC7);
  - live feedback with sound past the end still ends at `songEnd + lag`, with `overrunReadings` empty (AC8).

## F12-S04: The overrun lane

Story: [`F12-S04-overrun-lane`](../docs/features/12-score-after-the-song/stories/F12-S04-overrun-lane.md)

Covers: AC1, AC2, AC3, AC4, AC5, AC6, AC7, AC8, AC9

Placement decided at breakdown (2026-11-23, by the user): the overrun lane is its own short lane under the last line, left-aligned with that line's lane. Its time zero is the last note's end, and it uses the last line's average ms-to-x scale (lane width over the row's ms).

- [x] `src/app/overrun.ts`: `overrunTrace(readings, checks, startMs): { points: { timeMs, cents }[]; endMs } | null`. Points are the readings that pass the checks, with `timeMs` relative to `startMs` (the last note's end) and `cents` from `nearestPitch(frequency).cents` (AC2). Each point lasts until the next reading's time. `endMs` is the last point's end: the next reading's time, or its own when it is the last reading. The result is null with no point (AC4). Tests: silence only (null), a held note then silence (`endMs`), a few separate notes, a reading at the very end.
- [x] `src/app/overrun.ts`: `overrunLane(lastRow: LaneRow, layoutWidth, layoutHeight, trace, tempo, tolerance)` gives every view-box figure the overlay needs:
  - `x0`/`x1` are the last row's first and last stop x, and the scale is `(x1 - x0) / ((to - from) * 60000 / tempo)` px per ms (AC1).
  - The lane sits under `layoutHeight` with a small label band above it, and is the last row's lane height.
  - `extraHeight` is the view-box height to add for it.
  - `paths: RowPaths` holds one `TRACE_THICKNESS` rectangle per point at `traceY(cents, ...)` in `traceColour(cents, tolerance)`, plus `guides` at ±tolerance and ±`RIGHT_NOTE_BAND_CENTS` (AC2).
  - `more` is null, or the ms past the visible span when `endMs` exceeds it; points beyond the span are cut at `x1` (AC3).
  - `overrunMoreText(ms)` in `judge-text.ts` gives "+3.2 s".
  - Tests: x and y of points, colours, the cut and `more`, a trace inside the span.
- [x] `src/app/App.tsx`: memoise `overrun = after && timed.phase === "summary" ? overrunTrace(timed.overrunReadings, checks, lastNoteEndMs) : null`. `checks` is `{ ...DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS, band: BAND }`, and `lastNoteEndMs` is the timeline's last note `endMs`. Pass it in the `song` view (`overrun`) and to `Card` as `timedOverrunMs` (its `endMs`). A restart empties `overrunReadings`, so both go (AC7).
- [x] `src/app/Staff.tsx`: `SongStaff` computes the lane from `view.overrun` and the last `LaneRow`. When there is one, it sets the box's `aspect-ratio` to `width / (height + extraHeight)` (a separate effect from the draw, so the staff is not redrawn) and passes the lane to `SongOverlay`. The music SVG is a top-aligned block, so it does not move. The box stays in flow above the sticky bar, so the lane scrolls clear of it at the page end (AC8). `src/render/staff` is not changed.
- [x] `src/app/SongOverlay.tsx`: take `overrun` (the lane or null). Extend the `viewBox` height by its `extraHeight`. Draw a lane rectangle (`.lane-graded`), its guides (`.lane-guides`), the three colour paths (`fill-*`), a `<text class="overrun-label">` "after the end" (`OVERRUN_LABEL` in `judge-text.ts`) at the left of the label band, and, when `more` is set, a right-aligned `<text>` with `overrunMoreText` (AC1, AC2, AC3). Nothing is drawn when it is null (AC4). Halo styling as the spot label in `app.css`.
- [x] `src/app/analysis-text.ts`: `overrunText(ms)` gives "You played N s past the end", with N `(ms / 1000).toFixed(1)` (AC5). `Analysis.tsx` takes `overrunMs?: number | null` and adds it as the last item of the habits list, after the drift line, rendering the habits `h3` when this is its only line. `Card` passes `timedOverrunMs` through. Tests in `tests/app/analysis-text.test.ts`: short (0.3 s), long (12.4 s), and the rounding.

## Notes

- **Same result as live:** in after the song, the run is fed only readings up to the last slot's end (the point where live feedback finishes), so later readings go to the overrun alone. The last slot's end equals the last note's end, because `songSlotsMs` gives the last note its own length.
- **The analysis line is app text,** not a field of `src/core/analysis`: the overrun readings are not in the segments `analyse` reads, and the line is only a duration.
- **Legend** shows in after the song only with the lanes at the end; it is hidden in ready, the count-in and while playing.
- **The quiet line** shows through the count-in too (the card's `playing` phase), and "Listening…" shows from the last slot's end, including the one-lag wait when the player is already silent.
- **Staff module unchanged:** the overrun lane's room comes from growing the box's aspect ratio and the overlay's view box, not from the VexFlow layout.
