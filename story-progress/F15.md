## Notes

- **Where the constants live.** The design left this to the breakdown. `EDGE_GRACE_MS` moves to `song` beside `RELEASE_SHARE` and `RELEASE_CAP_QUARTERS`, and coverage re-exports it, so the floor and the grace are one number, `song` imports nothing from `coverage`, and every existing import of `EDGE_GRACE_MS` from coverage keeps working. The other scoring constants stay in coverage.
- **One span rule.** `scoredSpan` in coverage (S01) is used by both coverage and the analysis (S02). A note's `releaseMs` is 0 exactly when its end is slurred into the next note, so the span needs only the slurred start from the caller.
- **The release is worked out at the timeline's tempo**, not `song.tempo`: the fixtures build at ♩ = 60 with `tempo: null`, and the app builds at `song.tempo ?? DEFAULT_TEMPO`. Playback uses the same fallback, so lane and tone agree (S04 AC5).
- **In-tune share counts right sound in the release**, so a full-length note's in-tune share is today's (S01 AC4) and the breakdown's in-tune and out-of-tune time match coverage (S02 AC5).
- **Written-end marks** need `laneNoteEnds`, because a row holding only a note's release has no segment (S03 AC3) but can hold its written end (S03 AC5).
- **Tiny notes** (under about 53 ms) are outside every story's examples; the playback guard keeps a tone positive and the lane draws nothing for them.
- Not changed: the glossary has "edge grace" but no "release allowance" entry. Adding one is a `plan/` change, if wanted.
